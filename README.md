## Overview

> **Klinische Präzision trifft ästhetische Empathie.**
> 60 Jahre gebündeltes Wissen in Treatments, Masterclasses & Artist Training.

The platform for **Glamour Akademie**, a Hamburg continuing-education academy for the aesthetics industry. Its audience is not end clients but working professionals — Heilpraktikerinnen, Ärztinnen and ambitious practitioners deciding where to spend their training budget.

### Key features

- **Masterclasses** — the flagship intensive formats, each presented with its own depth
- **Single modules** — individually bookable units for practitioners building a skill set piece by piece
- **Voice assistant** — a conversational ElevenLabs-powered guide that answers course questions in natural speech instead of routing everyone to a contact form
- **Enquiry and booking path** — a direct route from a course page to a conversation with the academy
- **GSAP scroll choreography** — sequenced reveals that pace the page like a prospectus
- **Editorial typography** — Playfair Display against Outfit, set on a warm bone palette for a register closer to a medical institute than a beauty brand
- **German-language, GDPR-conscious** — German throughout, with a proper Datenschutz page

## Built for

**Glamour Akademie — The Perfect Skin®**, the education arm of the Mesoskin Hamburg practice, under the aesthetic direction of **Annette Fascher-Wendlandt**. The curriculum covers infusion therapy, lips, PDO threads and mesotherapy, taught hands-on in Hamburg and through 1:1 mentoring in the practitioner's own clinic.

### The brief

Selling training to practitioners is a different problem from selling treatments to clients. The buyer is an expert, sceptical by trade, and choosing between competing academies on the strength of who's teaching and how close the training gets to real practice. The site needed to:

- Address professionals as peers — no consumer-beauty gloss, no promises the room can't back up
- Make the distinction between masterclasses and single modules obvious, since they're different commitments and different budgets
- Put the teaching lineage and the sixty years of accumulated practice at the centre, because that is the actual product
- Answer the specific, technical questions a practitioner has before booking — without forcing every one of them through an email thread
- Stand on its own, separate from the clinical practice it grew out of, while clearly belonging to the same house

The voice assistant exists for that fourth point: practitioners ask narrow, specific questions about technique, prerequisites and format, and a spoken answer converts better than a contact form nobody fills in.
